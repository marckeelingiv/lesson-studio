# Purchase evidence, before the answer

**Status:** business-first script and interactive frontend implemented; browser/capture review and live evidence verification pending. No outcome evaluation or business insight recorded.

## Goal

Help engineers and hiring contacts assess how a business question becomes inspectable evidence: Question → Evidence → Definition → Answer → Trust / limits. Demonstrate engineering judgment rather than unverified throughput or a fabricated chart.

- [Portable five-scene script](presenter.json), 105 seconds. Import into the existing presenter if useful; it is independent of the interactive dashboard.
- [Dashboard operating guide](../../../lesson_studio/README.md#interactive-evidence-desk): the standalone evidence desk controls a clean audience window via a sanitized same-origin bridge. Do not embed two independently interactive dashboard iframes.
- Subject evidence contract and prerequisites: [DE for Fun evidence-dashboard.md at d59530c](https://github.com/marckeelingiv/de-for-fun/blob/d59530c/docs/evidence-dashboard.md). Subject owns its API, artifacts, services, data and technical evidence.
- The proposed purchase-pattern question must be narrowed against observed columns, grain, measures and a real Lightdash connection before claiming an answer. No metric, trend, chart or customer impact is assumed here.

## Preparation gate

1. Explicitly prepare the subject companion with an existing manifest and saved snapshot; record the exact subject commit and selected artifact labels/hashes. Nothing in the studio generates artifacts or starts the companion.
2. Inspect the purchase branch and another branch; compare actual observed counts/schema with current source and compiled manifest SQL. Preserve missing-column and SQL-reference warnings.
3. Complete the subject's Lightdash project/dashboard prerequisites separately. Until then, the Answer step is a purposeful setup handoff, not an answered business question.
4. Check clean-window synchronization, keyboard column focus/pin, SQL legibility, service state labels and private-control exclusion in an actual browser/capture rehearsal. Automated checks do not establish these visual outcomes.
5. Record the dashboard/script commit, actual artifact provenance, chosen question and verified result (if any) before publishing.
