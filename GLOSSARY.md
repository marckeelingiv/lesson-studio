# Lesson Video Production

Canonical editing and production vocabulary for planning, sifting, storyboarding, compositing, reviewing, and delivering educational engineering videos.

## Language

### Footage and Timeline

**A-Roll**:
Primary narrative footage containing the speaker or main walkthrough and the primary spoken audio.
_Avoid_: A-cut, main video, talking-head track

**B-Roll**:
Supplemental footage, screen captures, or visual inserts placed over A-roll to support what is being said.
_Avoid_: cutaway footage, filler clips

**Rough Cut**:
An edited timeline after sifting, with structure and pacing set before visual polish, motion plates, or sound design.
_Avoid_: radio edit, A-cut, first assembly, sifted cut

**Picture Lock**:
The milestone where all cuts, scene layouts, and visual timings are finalized so sound design, music mix, and final captions lock to exact frames.
_Avoid_: visuals locked, locked cut

**J-Cut / L-Cut**:
A split edit where the next clip's audio starts before its video (J-cut) or the current clip's audio continues over the next clip's video (L-cut).
_Avoid_: audio overlap, split edit

### Editorial Process

**Creative Brief**:
Per-video creative direction defining one to two reference examples (good vs. not good), intensity dials, emphasized moments, and starting visual anchors.
_Avoid_: edit prompt, video spec, vibe doc

**Sift**:
The deterministic first editorial pass that syncs tracks and removes dead space, retakes, and unwanted filler words from raw takes.
_Avoid_: pacing edit, cleanup pass, silence removal

**Dead Space**:
Non-speaking gaps between words that exceed natural breathing room and hold no intentional visual activity.
_Avoid_: dead air, silence, empty gap

**Room Tone**:
The baseline ambient sound of a recording space, measured per take to separate speech from dead space.
_Avoid_: noise floor, ambient noise, background hiss

**Consonant Tail**:
The quiet final acoustic release of a spoken word (especially `S`, `T`, `D`, `K`, and `P`) preserved so cuts do not clip speech.
_Avoid_: word tail, trailing audio, decay

### Visuals and Framing

**Storyboard**:
A beat-by-beat plan mapping rough-cut timecodes to scene layouts, motion plates, and recurring visual variations.
_Avoid_: visual plan, shot plan

**Scene Layout**:
A named framing arrangement of speaker and visuals (`Full Screen`, `Panel`, `Centre`, `Full Graphic`, `Circle`).
_Avoid_: composition, framing mode, screen layout

**Motion Plate**:
A modular, self-contained animated graphic, interface reveal, or diagram composited onto the timeline for a specific beat.
_Avoid_: graphic cue, visual card, animated overlay, B-roll card

**Contact Sheet**:
A static grid of rendered keyframe stills for planned scenes and motion plates, reviewed before full video rendering.
_Avoid_: keyframe preview, visual deck, storyboard frames

**Safe Zone**:
Frame boundary margins protecting captions, speaker face, and screen readability from clipping or overlap.
_Avoid_: margin box, caption zone

### Quality and Feedback

**Flash Frame**:
An unintended one-to-two-frame visual artifact caused by misaligned cuts or transitions.
_Avoid_: stray frame, cut glitch

**Revision Pass**:
A focused review-and-edit iteration targeting a small batch of specific creative or technical notes.
_Avoid_: 20-shotting, tweak loop, polish round

**Taste Profile**:
A persistent, tagged record of review feedback (`DO`, `DON'T`, `PREFERENCE`, `NON-NEGOTIABLE`) used to compound editing rules across videos.
_Avoid_: memory file, preference log, editor notes
