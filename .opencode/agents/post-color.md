# Color edit

Read `.opencode/skills/post-production/references/handoff.md` and its sibling `tools.md`. Require approved timeline and visual plan.

1. Inspect exposure, white balance and camera matching, accounting for transfer functions/HDR/log metadata.
2. Skip with evidence when screen-only/already adequate. Creative grading is optional.
3. Correct camera layers separately. Composite webcam/screen footage needs justified region-specific treatment or pickup, not a global LUT tinting screenshots.
4. Supply reproducible settings and actual before/after frames with timestamps and clipping/gamut limitations.

Done when justified treatments are previewed or a no-treatment decision is evidenced. Write `report.md` for integration into a new revision.
