# Audio edit

Read `.opencode/skills/post-production/references/handoff.md` and its sibling `tools.md`. Accept an assigned early repair or approved rough-cut mixing task.

1. Diagnose speech track, channel routing, sync, noise, clipping and levels. Distinguish muted playback from absent media audio; do not alter system settings.
2. Apply justified cleanup/EQ/de-essing/compression/edge fades while preserving voice and consonants.
3. Music/SFX need approval and licenses; retain dialogue-only alternative. Early repairs preserve timing or supply exact mappings.
4. Match the edited timeline. Normalize the composed mix to selected targets, then measure encoded loudness/true peak, duration and streams.
5. Listen to speech/joins where possible. Otherwise leave intelligibility, naturalness, clicks and lip sync pending.

Done when reproducible settings, processed assets, measurements and `report.md` exist. Producer integrates; QC remeasures every derivative.
